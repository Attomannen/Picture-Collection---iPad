Layer stack

One master, many looks

A crate panel with a base paint layer, rust underneath (wear, mask R), grime on top (dirt, mask G) and bright scratches (mask B). Pick a preset or build your own. The three presets are three instances of one material.

Switching a layer off here is the Use ... Layer static switch. In Unreal it removes the layer and its texture samples from the shader.

RGB mask

Four jobs, one texture

This is what you paint in Substance Painter on the unique UVs. R is wear, G is dirt, B is scratches and A is baked ambient occlusion. Click a tile to view that channel on the panel above. Stored together they look like the magenta and blue mask sheets in the assignment.

Height-aware blend

From a soft mask to a chipped edge

A 1k mask is blurry. MF_RevealWeight uses the mask as a water level and the layer's height map plus a breakup noise as the terrain: pixels switch on in the order of the terrain, so the edge follows real texture detail.

Try Softness 0.02 with Height Influence 1 and Breakup 0.6 for razor-sharp, irregular chips. Height 0.5 and noise 0.5 are the neutral values. Watch the curve: at the defaults a mask value of 0.3 reveals only a few pixels and 0.7 almost all of them, so paint your masks clearly white where the effect must show.

Vertex paint

Paint black, reveal the brick

The assignment's setup is Mesh Paint, Vertex Color tab, Red channel only, Paint Color black and Erase Color white. Drag on the wall to paint. The dots are the mesh's vertices. Paint only exists where vertices are, so a denser mesh paints finer.

Switch to Plain gradient to see the same paint without height and breakup. It is a blurry smear. The painted amount is 1 minus vertex red, because an unpainted mesh is white.

Cheat sheet

The knobs you will use most

ParameterWhat it changes
Base TintRecolours the paint layer. The fastest way to get a new instance.
Wear IntensityHow much of mask R counts as worn. Above 1 grows the damage, below 1 keeps only the worst edges.
Wear SoftnessWidth of the chipped edge. Small is crisp, large is a smooth fade.
Wear Height InfluenceHow much the rust texture's own height decides where chips appear first. Negative flips it.
Wear BreakupErodes the edge with the tiling breakup noise, so a 1k mask stays sharp up close.
Wear Edge StrengthDark rim where paint meets the exposed layer.
Wear Invert0 uses mask R as painted, 1 turns it inside out (the same for Dirt and Scratch Invert).
Layer TintsAll white by default: a texture you assign shows exactly as authored. A colour multiplies it.
Dirt AO BoostAdds grime in crevices using the baked AO in mask alpha.
Global TilingTexel-density knob for every tiling texture at once.
Use ... (group 00)Static switches. Off means the layer and its texture samples are gone from the shader.
Vertex 1 Softness / Height Influence / BreakupSharpens and roughens the painted edge, exactly as the playground above.
Debug View + Debug ModeShows masks, weights, vertex colour and UV checks on the mesh. Start here when something looks wrong.
